Questions people ask

Is my financial data safe?

We decide together what the tool can see and where it runs. I ask for the least access the job needs, and secrets never live in the code.

Do you do bookkeeping?

I build the tools that make bookkeeping easier. My own bookkeeping extension at Crossroads is still a work in progress, so for filing and tax work, keep your accountant.

What if the tool breaks later?

Every tool comes with notes on what it does and how to check it. Ongoing care is available if you want someone to keep an eye on it.
