Websites and online stores

A website that answers the phone for you

Most people who find your business online want one of four things: your hours, your address, a price, or a way to book or buy. I build sites that put those first and send each visitor to one clear next step.

Is this for you?

  • You have no website, or one you are embarrassed to send people to.
  • Customers keep calling to ask things the site should already answer.
  • You sell or book through Square, Shopify, Toast, Vagaro or a similar tool, and the website does not connect to it.
  • You want to be found when someone in Columbus searches for what you do.

What you get

One page per thing you sell

Each service gets its own page with its own title, so it can show up in search on its own.

A clear call to action

Quote, book, order, call or visit. One main action per page, tested on a phone before launch.

Your tools, connected

Buttons go straight to the booking, ordering or checkout page you already pay for. No second system to keep in sync.

Search basics done right

Titles, descriptions, a sitemap, structured data and a page that loads fast on a phone.

Facts you can trust

Hours, address and prices come from you. Nothing on the page is made up, including reviews.

The files are yours

You get the source and a short note on how to change the basics yourself.

Questions people ask

How long does a small business website take?

A one page site with a clear call to action can be ready to preview within days once I have your facts and photos. A larger site with several service pages or a store takes longer. I give you a timeline before we start.

Can you work with the booking or ordering system I already have?

Usually, yes. If your provider gives you a public booking, ordering or shop page, the site links straight to it. If you want a cart on your own site, I check what your platform allows first.

Will my site show up on Google?

I build every page so search engines can read it and so each service has its own page. Nobody can promise a ranking. I can set up Google Search Console so you can see what people actually search for.

Do I have to use AI to work with you?

No. I use AI tools to build faster, and you get a normal website you can edit or hand to anyone.

Ready when you are.

A line or two is plenty. I’ll get back to you about what I can do, how long it might take, and what it would cost.
