Marketing

One offer, one page, posts that point to it

Posting more is not a plan. A campaign that works picks one offer people understand, gives it a page with one next step, and tracks which posts brought in real inquiries.

Is this for you?

  • You post on Instagram or Facebook but cannot tell if it brings in business.
  • You have a new service, a slow season or an event to fill.
  • You need print pieces, like a brochure or flyer, that match what is online.
  • You want someone to do the work, not just hand you a strategy deck.

What you get

An offer in one sentence

What it is, who it is for and what to do next.

A landing page

Posts and print

A set of Instagram and Facebook posts, plus brochures or flyers when they help.

Tracking you can read

Tagged links per channel and a simple source field for messages and calls.

Do you run paid ads?

No. Everything we post is true and uses photos you have permission to share.

How do I know it worked?

We agree on the goal first, such as five qualified inquiries in two weeks, and count them by channel. The campaign tutorial shows the method.
