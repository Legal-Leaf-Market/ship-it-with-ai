Ongoing care

Keep it running after launch

Websites and tools don’t break all at once. A form stops sending, a payment link changes, a plugin updates, and nobody notices for two weeks. Ongoing care means someone is watching, and you have one person to call.

Is this for you?

  • Your site or tool takes orders, bookings or inquiries and you can’t afford for it to quietly stop.
  • Nobody on your team wants to be “the computer person”.
  • You want small changes made without starting a new project each time.
  • You had something built, by me or someone else, and want it looked after.

What you get

Regular checks

Forms, checkout and booking links tested on a schedule, the way a customer would use them.

Small changes

New hours, a new service, a price change, a seasonal page. Ask and it gets done.

Updates and backups

Software kept current, with the last working version ready to restore.

Renewals in view

Domains, hosting and outside services tracked, so nothing lapses by surprise.

One person to call

When something looks off, you call me, not a help desk.

Still yours

Care is optional. The code, logins and hosting stay in your name, and the fix keeps working if you stop.

Questions people ask

What does ongoing care cost?

It is a monthly plan, sized to what you have and how much changes. I quote it with the project, or on its own if someone else built your site.

Do I need a care plan?

No. You own what I build and it works without one. Care is for owners who would rather not think about it.

Can you look after a site someone else built?

Usually, yes. I take a look first and tell you honestly what shape it is in.

Ready when you are.

A line or two is plenty. I’ll get back to you about what I can do, how long it might take, and what it would cost.
