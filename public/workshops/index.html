Ship your first website

90 minutes · Up to 12 people

For small-business owners and teams who want to take an idea from a short brief to a working one-page site. Bring a laptop, a browser, and one idea you can describe in a sentence. Jake will confirm the accounts and hosting setup before your session.

What we’ll do

  1. First 15 minutes: define the visitor, goal, and call to action.
  2. Next 30 minutes: build the page from a brief and review the first draft.
  3. Next 25 minutes: test links, copy, and the mobile layout.
  4. Final 20 minutes: publish where access is ready, or leave with a working preview and a launch checklist.

Take home your source files, a reusable brief, and a checklist for the next launch.
