Built for my own business

How we run Crossroads Wedding Co.

The shoemaker’s kids do have shoes. Crossroads is my wedding and events business, and we use software we built in-house to manage bookings. I know which screens matter because I use the system myself.

  1. Inquiries and bookings

    Capture what the couple needs and keep their details with the booking.

  2. Agreements and deposits

    Keep the service agreement, signatures, and deposit status tied to the event.

  3. Transactions and books

    I’m extending that setup into in-house bookkeeping so we can track transactions and see how the business is doing.

AI makes this kind of custom build practical for a small team. For your business, I’d start with a workflow you already know well and build around the way you actually work.
