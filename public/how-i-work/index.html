How I work

From “this takes too long” to working software

Small steps you can see, a working preview before anything goes live, and a price before the work starts.

  1. Tell me what’s stuck

    Send a line or two through the form. What you do, and what you wish worked better.

  2. Free session and prototype

    I sit with the person who does the job for two to four hours, then build a working prototype around how they do it. Free.

  3. A fixed quote

    What the real version would take and what it costs. If you don’t need me, I will say so.

  4. You try it first

    Before anything goes live, you use it on a real task. We fix what doesn’t fit.

  5. We launch together

    We check it on a phone and a computer, test every button, and keep the last working version ready to restore.

  6. You keep the keys

    Who owns the work is agreed in writing. You get the source files, logins stay in your name, and you get short notes on changing the basics.

What I won’t do

  • Make up reviews, ratings, customers or facts about your business.
  • Lock you into a platform or hold your files hostage.
  • Put something live you haven’t tried first.
  • Sell you software you don’t need. If you don’t need me, I’ll say so.
  • Make Betty bend or break to fit the software. The fix fits how she works.

Questions people ask

How much does a project cost?

The first consultation and prototype are free. After that every build gets a fixed quote before it starts, so there are no surprise invoices. Ongoing care is a monthly plan, quoted with the project.

Who owns the website or tool?

We agree on it in writing before I start. You get the source files, and the logins and hosting stay in your name. No lock-in.

Do you use AI to build?

Yes, it is in the name. AI tools let me build and test faster. I review everything before you see it, and nothing goes live without your approval.

Do you meet in person?

Yes, in Columbus, Indiana and nearby. Online works too.

What if I only need one small thing fixed?

That is a fine place to start. Plenty of good projects begin with one broken form or one spreadsheet.

Will you post or send anything in my name without asking?

No. You approve every page, post and message before it goes out.

Ready when you are.

A line or two is plenty. I’ll get back to you about what I can do, how long it might take, and what it would cost.
