Free consultation and prototype

I’ll sit with Betty. Free.

Give me two to four hours with the person who does the slow, manual job. I watch how she does it, then build a working prototype around her process. No charge, no pitch, nothing to sign up for.

Every business has a Betty

She’s been there for years. She knows where every number comes from and why the report has to be built the way it is. Half your processes live in her head.

She’s also tired. The tools she was handed make her copy, paste, retype and double-check, so the job that should take twenty minutes takes all afternoon.

Betty isn’t the problem. The system she works in is. Most fixes make Betty learn a new platform from scratch. I do the opposite: keep what works for her, fix what doesn’t, and don’t make her bend or break to fit the software.

How the free session works

  1. Tell me the job

    A short note: what takes too long and who carries it.

  2. I sit with Betty

    Two to four hours beside her on a normal day, with her permission. I watch and take notes.

  3. Two lists

    What Betty wants fixed, in her words, and what I noticed that she has stopped seeing.

  4. A working prototype

    Something you can click, built on sample data, shown to Betty first.

  5. Your call

    Keep the notes and the idea. If you want the real thing, I quote a fixed price.

Why I can do this in an afternoon

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The visit and the prototype cost you nothing, and you are not signing up for anything. If you want the real version built, I quote a fixed price first and you decide.

What do you need from me?

Two to four hours of one person’s time, a task they do every week, and permission to watch it. Sample or scrubbed data is fine for the prototype.

Who is Betty?

Every business has one: the person who keeps the office running and knows where every number comes from. She is never the problem. The tools she was handed are.

Will you make us switch software?

Not unless you want to. I start with the tools you already use and build around how the work is done today.

What happens to what you see?

It stays between us. Your processes and numbers belong to you and are never shared with another client.

Do you come to us?

In Columbus, Indiana and nearby, yes. Farther away, we can do it online with screen sharing.
