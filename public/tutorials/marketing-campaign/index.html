Plan and execute a marketing campaign

Build a two-week Instagram and Facebook campaign that connects activity to inquiries.

On this page

  1. Choose an offer and a goal
  2. Prepare the destination
  3. Write and schedule the posts
  4. Review the results

1. Choose an offer and a goal

Use one offer and one action so you can tell what worked. A fictional repair shop could offer an estimate for loose dining chairs, with a goal of five qualified inquiries in two weeks. Define “qualified” in advance, such as a local customer with a repair your shop handles.

2. Prepare the destination

Link to a page that repeats the exact offer, explains what the customer should send, and has one inquiry action. Use separate campaign tags for each channel where you can measure them. If you accept messages directly, give staff a simple source field to fill in.

Instagram: ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=chair_repair
Facebook: ?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=chair_repair

3. Write and schedule the posts

In week one, show the problem and a repair example. In week two, answer a question about pricing or turnaround and repeat the estimate invitation. On Instagram, give the image or demonstration the lead. On Facebook, include the service area and explain how to ask for an estimate. Use photos you have permission to share.

4. Review the results

Track qualified inquiries and bookings by channel alongside visits. If a post gets clicks but few inquiries, test its landing page and call to action. If inquiries come in but rarely book, check offer fit and follow-up time. Change one thing in the next round so you can see what helped.
